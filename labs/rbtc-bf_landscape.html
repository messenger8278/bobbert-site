02 / 2026 outlook

Approximately 9,500 applications.

A planning estimate based on statewide momentum.

Virginia recorded 129,126 business applications in January–October 2026, compared with 117,602 in the same reporting months of 2025, an increase of 9.8%. These totals use the not seasonally adjusted, all-industry Business Applications series.

Calculation: 8,650 × (129,126 ÷ 117,602) = 9,497.6, rounded to approximately 9,500.

This assumes Region 2’s full-year growth follows Virginia’s year-to-date growth. Monthly Census figures are available for Virginia; Region 2’s constituent counties and independent cities are reported annually. This is an analyst extrapolation, not an official Census projection or a count of operating businesses.

For context, continuing Region 2’s 2022–2025 compound annual growth rate of 4.9% produces approximately 9,100 applications. The 9,100–9,500 span compares two methods; it is not a statistical confidence interval.

2025 includes 53 reporting weeks, while 2022–2024 each include 52. The statewide year-to-date ratio is applied without a separate calendar adjustment.

04 / Method & sources

What the numbers measure.

We analyze large public datasets for regional insights. The underlying business application data are collected and published by the U.S. Census Bureau, not collected or owned by Chip Bobbert. Aggregation, visualizations, interpretations, and the labeled projection are independent analysis; the Census Bureau does not sponsor or endorse this page.

  • Measure: Business applications, not confirmed business openings, new employers, or jobs created.
  • Geography: Counties of Alleghany, Amherst, Appomattox, Bedford, Botetourt, Campbell, Craig, Floyd, Franklin, Giles, Montgomery, Pulaski, and Roanoke; cities of Covington, Lynchburg, Radford, Roanoke, and Salem.
  • Historical release: June 10, 2026 Census annual county data; all four historical years come from that release, including revised earlier-year counts. Counts include disclosure-avoidance noise and may be revised.
  • Monthly release: November 12, 2026, covering October 2026. Virginia year-to-date totals compare the same January–October reporting months.
  • Reporting calendar: 2022: January 2–December 31, 2022; 2023: January 1–December 30, 2023; 2024: December 31, 2023–December 28, 2024; 2025: December 29, 2024–January 3, 2026. The first three contain 52 weeks; 2025 contains 53.
