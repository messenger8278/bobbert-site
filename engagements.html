Engagements

Build better programs. Make clearer decisions. Move ideas forward.

How I can help

Ways to work together

01

Program & Learning Design

Turn ambitious ideas into experiences that work.

Design or strengthen innovation and entrepreneurship programs, curricula, workshops, and learning experiences, from the first concept through implementation.

A typical engagement begins with your audience, goals, and existing offerings. Together, we develop learning objectives, curriculum, activities, and a plan for piloting and improvement.

02

Executive Advising

A thinking partner for consequential decisions.

Work through strategy, organizational challenges, and new opportunities with an outside perspective and a practical path forward.

A typical engagement includes confidential conversations once or twice a month. We work through current challenges, test ideas, and clarify decisions, with follow-up support between sessions as agreed.

03

Program Evaluation

Understand what’s working, and what to do next.

Clarify program goals, assess outcomes, and turn evidence into decisions about what to improve, expand, or rethink.

A typical engagement is a focused project: we define evaluation questions, review existing evidence, gather additional data as needed, and deliver findings and practical recommendations.

04

Startup Advising

Move from promising idea to informed action.

Get help refining your concept, testing assumptions, setting priorities, and navigating product and technology decisions.

A typical engagement centers on regular working sessions around your venture’s most pressing questions. We identify assumptions, plan customer or product experiments, review what you learn, and turn those insights into next steps.

05

Fractional CTO & CIO

Make technology work for your organization, and its future.

Get senior, part-time leadership across technology strategy, product development, systems, and technical teams. I help align technology decisions and investments with your organization’s goals.

A typical engagement begins with an assessment of your technology, systems, team, and organizational needs. I help establish a roadmap, guide development and investment decisions, support staff and vendors, and lead implementation.

06

Facilitation

Bring fresh thinking to the room.

I help groups work through questions about innovation, entrepreneurship, learning, and technology. Bring me into your next strategic retreat to challenge assumptions, explore possibilities, and turn discussion into shared direction.

A typical engagement includes a planning conversation, a tailored agenda, and a facilitated workshop or retreat session. I can join for a focused session or a full day, helping your group leave with clear priorities and next steps.

07

Interim Leadership & Support

Keep important work moving through a staffing transition.

When a key staff member leaves, I can step in to fill the gap, support your team, and keep programs and projects on track while you determine what comes next.

A typical engagement begins with a rapid handoff and agreement on immediate priorities. I take on defined responsibilities, stabilize the work, and help prepare for a smooth transition to your next hire.

08

Customer Discovery Facilitator

Learn what matters to the people you hope to serve.

I bring extensive experience conducting customer discovery interviews that uncover needs, motivations, and how people make decisions. Together, we turn those conversations into evidence for your next move.

A typical engagement includes defining learning goals, developing an interview guide, and conducting interviews with customers or potential users. I synthesize the findings into patterns, implications, and recommendations for what to test next.
