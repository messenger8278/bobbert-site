01 / About Me

About Me

I've spent 25 years working across education, innovation, technology, and entrepreneurship.

My work spans program development, emerging technologies, educational design, funding, and economic development. I currently serve as Director of Innovation at the Roanoke Blacksburg Innovation Alliance and Roanoke Blacksburg Technology Council.

Previous work includes the Colopy Entrepreneurship Program at the North Carolina School of Science and Mathematics, Stand Together, Duke University, and UNC Wilmington. I also co-founded Construct3D.
